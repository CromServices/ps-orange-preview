About Peter and Sue

A family owned Toowoomba building business.

Peter Orange first began his building career in 1981 and has been a registered builder since 1994. Sue Orange takes care of all the office duties, internal designs and colour consulting.

We specialise in renovations and new builds, including bathrooms, built-in robes, kitchens and all carpentry work.

Our carpentry team consists of skilled tradesmen and apprentices. All other trades are supplied by licensed contractors with many years of experience — plumbers, electricians, tilers, plasterers and water-proofers.
