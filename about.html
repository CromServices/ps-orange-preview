About Peter and Sue

A family owned Toowoomba building business.

Your photo here

P & S Orange is a family owned and locally run building business. First established 35 years ago.

P & S Orange is owned and operated by Toowoomba locals Peter and Sue Orange. Head builder Peter got his start in the construction trade back in 1981 and became a registered builder in 1994. Sue Orange is responsible for all office duties as well as internal designs and colour consulting.

Peter and Sue, along with their skilled tradespeople, have built many new homes, units, commercial buildings and been involved in countless home renovations throughout the area.

We specialise in renovations and new builds, including bathrooms, built-in robes, kitchens and all carpentry work.

Our carpentry team consists of skilled tradesmen and apprentices. All other trades are supplied by licensed contractors with many years of experience — plumbers, electricians, tilers, plasterers and water-proofers.
